Connectome-to-Behavior: Closed-Loop Drosophila Emulation[cite: 2]
Ongoing · GitHub Repo
→
Closed-loop simulation coupling complete Drosophila connectomes (FlyWire, BANC) with Brian2 point-neuron dynamics and an embodied biomechanical body (MuJoCo, flygym) to evaluate emergent locomotion, grooming, and feeding without hand-coded motor rules[cite: 2].
Research Question: Can natural coordinated behaviors emerge organically when real synaptic wiring is given simple biophysical rules and placed in an embodied physical simulation[cite: 2]?
Research Stack: Brian2 · FlyWire / BANC · MuJoCo · flygym · Python[cite: 2]

EM-NAV: Geometry & Transferability of Spatial Representations[cite: 8]
Completed · GitHub Repo
Completed →
Computational study examining how biological constraints (spiking thresholds, metabolic sparsity penalties, and recurrence) shape internal spatial representations, place-cell-like geometry, and zero-shot transfer stability in embodied agents under severe sensory ambiguity[cite: 8].
Key Finding: Constrained spiking networks produced place-cell-like coding with up to ~80x higher spatial information per spike than unconstrained baselines and demonstrated superior transferability into continuous 3D environments[cite: 8].
Research Stack: Python 3.10+ · snnTorch · PyTorch · Blender 3D · MiniGrid · PPO[cite: 8]
